Privacy Policy

Your reflections belong to you.

Lofi Memory is built as a calm place for personal notes, diary entries, reminders, games, and lofi breaks. This policy explains what information may be stored, why it is used, and how advertising and third-party services work.

Return to Lofi Memory

Data ownership and access

Lofi Memory is designed to be a private space. We do not have access to your private diary entries, notes, or reminders. Your personal writing is stored either in your local browser storage or securely in your personal Google-linked cloud account. We do not read, share, or use your private content for any purpose.

Information we collect

Journal and app content

If you write diary entries, notes, reminders, moods, habit items, custom labels, or memories, that content is used to provide the app features you choose. When you are not signed in, this information is generally stored in your browser through local storage.

Account information

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may provide a basic account identifier such as your email address or display name. This is used to keep your saved content connected to your account.

Technical information

Like most websites, Lofi Memory and its service providers may process browser, device, log, cookie, and usage information to keep the site working, protect the service, understand general performance, and support advertising.

How your information is used

To run the app

Information is used to save entries, restore settings, remember cookie consent, schedule reminders, sync signed-in content, and keep the lofi radio, games, notes, and journal tools working.

To improve reliability

We may use aggregated or technical information to understand bugs, loading issues, or product quality. We do not sell your private diary entries.

To support free access

Lofi Memory may show advertising through Google AdSense. Ads help support free access to the site.

Third-party services

Firebase

The site may use Firebase services such as Authentication, Firestore, and Messaging. These services can process account identifiers, stored app content, device tokens for notifications, and related technical data according to Google's policies.

Google AdSense

Google and its partners may use cookies, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google may use visits to this and other websites to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising. You can manage personalization at Google Ads Settings and review how Google uses data from partner sites at Google's partner sites policy page.

YouTube and embedded media

The lofi radio feature may use embedded YouTube content or related player technology. YouTube may process viewing, playback, cookie, and device information under Google's privacy practices.

Local storage, cookies, and notifications

Lofi Memory uses browser storage to remember settings such as theme choices, app preferences, cookie consent, and unsigned-in content. Cookies or similar technologies may also be used by authentication providers, embedded media providers, analytics or performance tools, and ad partners. The Advertising Policy explains how advertising should fit into the calmer user experience. Browser notifications are optional and only work when you allow them in your browser settings.

Your choices

Control saved content

You can delete your entries, notes, reminders, and memories inside the app where those tools are available. You can also clear local browser storage to remove unsigned-in data from the current device.

Control ads and cookies

You can change browser cookie settings, clear site data, use Google Ads Settings, or visit industry opt-out tools available in your region.

Contact us

For privacy questions or data requests, email atastymealy@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line.

Children and sensitive use

Lofi Memory is intended for general audiences and personal reflection. It is not a medical, therapy, crisis, or emergency service. If you are in danger or need urgent support, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.

Questions about privacy?

We aim to keep the policy clear and easy to find. If something is unclear, reach out anytime.
