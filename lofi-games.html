Why people look for lofi games

Most people searching for lofi games want a softer pace. They want something that works for a short reset, a quiet evening, or a background companion while they think, write, or decompress.

How Lofi Memory fits that mood

Instead of separating music, games, journaling, and to-dos into different tabs, Lofi Memory keeps them close together. That makes it easier to stay in the same relaxed headspace while you play, listen, and reset.
