A cozy website for music, games, and journaling.

Lofi Memory is a soft online space where you can listen to lofi music, play relaxing games, write privately, keep reminders, save memories, and come back to a calmer mood.

Why combine music, games, and writing?

Music sets the mood, games make the break feel light, and writing gives your thoughts somewhere to land. Keeping all three in one tab makes a small online break feel more complete.
