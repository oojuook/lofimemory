# Lofi Memory AdSense approval audit

Google says a site may be considered not ready to show ads if ad code is missing or incomplete, the site is unreachable, the site does not have enough unique content or a good user experience, or policy violations are present::cite[453].

Google also recommends reviewing pages before applying so the site has unique content that is relevant to visitors and provides a strong user experience::cite[570].

Google's AdSense help states that a privacy policy should explain how Google advertising cookies may be used on partner websites::cite[568].

AdSense eligibility requires that the publisher has their own content that meets Google's policies::cite[574].

## Current project judgment

Lofi Memory already has meaningful strengths for review: a real product, original content clusters, visible trust pages, a valid ads.txt file, and a reduced production surface that removes many weaker pages from the live build.

The biggest remaining risks are qualitative rather than technical:
- some indexed article pages still look more like lightweight SEO pages than strong standalone resources
- some indexed article pages do not surface trust, editorial, privacy, and contact links clearly enough
- the quality-control story is present, but it can be made more visible across reading pages and trust surfaces

## Priority actions in this pass

1. Add a visible trust-and-transparency block to indexed article pages.
2. Strengthen cross-linking between the blog, trust pages, and content quality policy.
3. Improve the content-quality policy page so it feels like a real review surface, not an orphan page.
4. Rebuild, verify, redeploy, and package the updated site.
