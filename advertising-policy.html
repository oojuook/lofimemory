Advertising policy

How advertising fits into Lofi Memory.

Lofi Memory is designed first as a calm place to listen to lofi music, relax, journal privately, keep notes, and play cozy games. Advertising, including Google AdSense if enabled, should support the website without making the experience noisy, misleading, or difficult to use.

Editorial independence

Articles, guides, game descriptions, and product explanations are written for visitors first. Ads do not decide what the site says, which games are recommended, or how privacy and wellbeing topics are explained. If a page discusses journaling, relaxing games, lofi music, or productivity, the content should remain useful even without ads.

Ad placement principles

  • Ads should not cover controls, block game play, interrupt writing, or make the page hard to read.
  • Ads should not be placed in ways that imitate buttons, navigation, journal controls, or system messages.
  • Low-value utility or legal pages, such as privacy and terms pages, are kept focused on information rather than ad density.
  • Pages with thin or duplicate content are not intended to be promoted as ad-heavy landing pages.

Privacy and personalized advertising

Google AdSense and related advertising technologies may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar signals to measure performance and personalize or limit ads depending on visitor settings, region, consent choices, and Google policies. The Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy explain how browser storage, cookies, embedded media, notifications, and advertising-related technologies fit together.

Content and policy safety

Lofi Memory avoids content designed to mislead visitors, encourage unsafe behavior, or create a poor ad experience. Wellbeing language is kept general and reflective rather than medical. Game and article pages should provide original context instead of existing only to target search keywords.

Questions or concerns

If an ad appears intrusive, misleading, broken, or out of place, visitors can use the Contact page to report it. Feedback helps keep the website calmer and more useful.

Last reviewed: October 2026. This policy may be updated as Lofi Memory changes its ad setup, content structure, or privacy disclosures.
