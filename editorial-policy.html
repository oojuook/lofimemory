Editorial Policy

Helpful first, search second.

Lofi Memory aims to publish calm, original, family-friendly content about journaling, relaxation, focus, music, and cozy browser breaks. This page explains how content is chosen, updated, and corrected.

Read the blog

What we publish

Original articles

We prioritize longer reads that explain a topic clearly, connect it to everyday life, and help visitors decide what to do next.

Product guidance

We also publish simple support and policy pages that explain how the site works, how privacy is handled, and how to contact the site owner.

Family-friendly tone

We avoid sensational framing and aim for language that feels calm, practical, and safe for general audiences.

How content is reviewed

Clarity check

Pages are reviewed for duplication, thin copy, and vague claims. If a page does not add enough standalone value, it should be merged, expanded, or removed from core promotion.

Accuracy check

Product and policy pages are reviewed against the current site behavior so visitors can understand what the app actually does before they sign in, write, or interact with ads.

Freshness check

Important trust pages are revisited when major site features change, especially around sign-in, storage, privacy, notifications, music embeds, and advertising.

Wellbeing boundary

Lofi Memory can talk about reflection, routines, focus, or calming breaks, but it is not a medical, therapy, crisis, or emergency service. Wellness language on the site is meant for general self-reflection, not diagnosis or treatment.

Advertising and monetization

When ads appear, they are intended to support free access to the site. Advertising does not change the editorial standard for helpfulness, accuracy, or family-friendly presentation. Policy, privacy, and support pages exist to help visitors understand the site before any monetization decision is made.

Corrections and updates

If you spot an outdated statement, broken page, or misleading description, please report it by email. Substantive fixes should be updated in the relevant page content rather than hidden in tiny footnotes.

Need a correction or clarification?

Send the page URL and a short note so the issue can be reviewed quickly.
