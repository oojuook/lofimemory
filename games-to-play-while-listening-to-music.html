Games + music

Games to play while listening to music online.

Lofi Memory is made for people who want one soft page where the music keeps playing and the games stay calm. Open the lofi radio, pick a cozy game, and relax without switching tabs.

Play with lofi music

Good games for music in the background

  • Solitaire for a familiar card-table reset
  • Sudoku and word games for quiet focus
  • Snake, Dinosaur Dash, and Lilypad Hopper for light movement
  • Lofi Jigsaw when you want the music and visuals to match

Why it feels better in one tab

Music, games, notes, and journaling stay together, so the break feels like a calm room instead of another distracting feed.
