Why a memories calendar helps

Sometimes you want more than a plain reminder. You want the date, the context, the feeling, and a little note about why it matters. That is what makes a memories calendar feel more personal than a generic alert list.

Why it fits Lofi Memory

Lofi Memory mixes reminders, notes, journaling, music, and mini games in one calmer environment, so your saved dates can sit near the rest of your day instead of being split across tools.
