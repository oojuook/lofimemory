Types of storage we use

Essential storage

We use browser storage to remember app preferences, design settings, saved local entries, game state, cookie consent, and other features that make the app usable.

Authentication storage

If you sign in, Firebase and Google may use cookies or local storage to keep your session secure and connect your content to your account.

Advertising storage

Google AdSense and advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, and measure ads.

Third-party cookies

Embedded YouTube media, Firebase, Google AdSense, and other third-party services may set or read cookies according to their own policies. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to Lofi Memory and other websites.

Your choices

You can accept the site's cookie notice, clear local browser data, block cookies in your browser, or adjust ad personalization at Google Ads Settings. Blocking some cookies or storage may affect sign-in, reminders, personalization, and saved local content.
