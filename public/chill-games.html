What makes a game feel chill?

Usually it is a mix of easy starts, warm visuals, short rounds, and low pressure. A chill game should help your attention settle instead of demanding intense focus from the first second.

What you can play here

  • Sudoku, sliding, and merge puzzles for tidy satisfying sessions
