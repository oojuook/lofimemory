Mood tracking

A mood tracker journal for noticing patterns gently.

A mood tracker does not need to feel clinical or heavy. Lofi Memory gives you a calm place to write what happened, mark how you felt, keep small reminders, revisit memories, and take a soft lofi game break before or after journaling.

Why mood tracking works better when it stays simple

Many people search for a mood tracker journal because they want to understand themselves without overthinking every entry. Lofi Memory keeps the experience gentle: name the feeling, add a few lines, and let the pattern build naturally over time.
