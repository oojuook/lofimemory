Relaxing games with lofi music and journaling in one calm browser tab.

Some breaks help you come back. Lofi Memory combines cozy games, soft music, and private journaling so a study break can become a reset instead of another feed loop.

The loop

Play one gentle round, listen to lofi music, write one honest sentence, then choose what happens next.

Why this combination works

Games lower friction

A familiar game gives your brain a simple starting point when you feel tired.

Lofi music sets the room

Soft audio makes the break feel contained and calm.

Journaling closes the loop

One sentence turns the break into reflection instead of drifting.

Try this 7-minute reset

  1. Minute 1: choose lofi music and breathe.
  2. Minutes 2-5: play one relaxing game.
  3. Minute 6: write what your mind needed.
  4. Minute 7: choose one next step: focus, rest, or close the tab.
