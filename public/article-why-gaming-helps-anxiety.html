Why mindless gaming helps relieve stress before journaling

Discover how simple, repetitive browser games act as a palate cleanser for your brain, reducing anxiety before you start writing.

The Palate Cleanser for Your Brain

When you are feeling overwhelmed, sitting down to write can feel like an impossible task. The blank page demands focus, but your mind is racing. This is where simple, repetitive "fidget games" come in.

Why Repetition Soothes Us

Games that require a steady rhythm—like keeping a leaf in the air or dodging obstacles in a slow-moving stream—engage just enough of your brain to distract you from your anxieties, but not enough to cause new stress. Psychologists refer to this as achieving a "flow state."
