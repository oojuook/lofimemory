Quality policy

Content quality and advertising readiness.

Lofi Memory is designed as an original, family-friendly browser experience for calm games, private journaling, lofi music, notes, reminders, and reflection. This page explains the standards used to keep the site useful and suitable for advertising-supported browsing.

What is indexed

The live site keeps a smaller, curated set of core pages available for review and indexing: the homepage, blog, trust pages, and longer original articles. Lower-value or overlapping narrow pages are not promoted in the sitemap and are removed from the production build until they are rewritten into genuinely useful resources.

Original and useful content

Pages are written to help visitors understand the product, choose a use case, or find a specific calm activity. Content is reviewed for overlap, similarity, usefulness, and whether it adds real context beyond a headline and a few generic paragraphs.

  • Product pages explain real features and use cases.
  • Guide pages focus on student routines, journaling, study breaks, and calm game use.
  • Trust pages explain editorial standards, advertising, privacy, and contact methods.

Review checklist before publishing

Before a page is kept in the live review surface, it should have a clear purpose, a readable title and description, enough original detail to help a real visitor, and visible navigation to trust pages. Pages that only repeat keywords, copy another page's structure too closely, or exist mainly to capture a search phrase are removed until they can be rewritten.

  • Each indexed page should answer a real visitor question or explain a real product area.
  • Internal links should point to pages that are actually live in the production build.
  • Wellbeing language should stay general, reflective, and non-clinical.
  • Ads should never hide navigation, writing tools, game controls, or policy information.

Family-friendly experience

The game room focuses on no-download, single-player, low-pressure games. The site avoids adult content, gambling, deceptive downloads, public chat, and other content that would conflict with a calm journaling and study-break product.

Transparent navigation and trust

Visitors can access key trust pages including About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, Editorial Policy, Advertising Policy, and Disclaimer. These pages are kept accessible through the site footer and supporting navigation.

Privacy-aware advertising

If advertising is displayed, it should not block core navigation, hide content, or make games and writing tools difficult to use. Ad placement should remain secondary to the product experience. Advertising-related disclosures are explained in the privacy and advertising policy pages.

Ongoing maintenance

The site is actively reviewed for broken links, layout issues, mobile usability, readable pages, and clear separation between content, tools, and advertising areas. When pages feel repetitive, low-value, or outdated, they should be revised, merged, or removed from indexing.

Contact and review

If something feels unclear, report it.

If you notice a misleading page, a trust issue, a broken link, or an advertising concern, please use the contact page so the issue can be reviewed directly.
