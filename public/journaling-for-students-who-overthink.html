Students & overthinking

Journaling for students who overthink before studying.

When your mind is loud, starting work can feel harder than the work itself. A short journal entry gives your thoughts somewhere to land before you open notes, music, or a Focus Tree session.

Open Focus TreeWrite first

Best routine

Write for two minutes, choose one study target, keep the Focus page visible, then close with one reflection.

Why students overthink before studying

Too many tasks

Assignments, deadlines, messages, and tabs compete for attention.

Starting feels vague

When the first step is unclear, scrolling becomes easier than studying.

Stress loops repeat

Writing the loop down helps stop it from repeating silently.

Five prompts before studying

  1. The one thing I need to study next is...
  2. The worry I can park for now is...
  3. If I only do 20 minutes, I will...
  4. I will know this session worked if...
  5. After studying, I want to feel...
