Try this 3-minute anxious-thought journal routine

1. Name the situation

Write what happened in plain language, without judging yourself for the reaction.

2. Separate fact from fear

Make two quick lines: “What I know” and “What my mind is predicting.”

3. Choose one kind next step

End with one tiny action: drink water, send the message, take a break, or close the loop for tonight.
