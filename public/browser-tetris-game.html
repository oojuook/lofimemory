Inspired by classic Tetris progression

Classic Tetris games are satisfying because the challenge grows over time. Quiet Tetris now follows that idea: the game starts at a calmer pace, then increases falling speed as your cleared lines push you into higher levels. The score also rewards line clears more as the level climbs.
