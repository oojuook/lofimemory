Quiet Tetris

A browser Tetris game with cozy level pacing.

Quiet Tetris inside Lofi Memory keeps the colorful block-stacking loop familiar while making it softer for a chill browser break. Clear lines, watch the level rise every 10 lines, and feel the pieces fall faster as the board gets more focused.

Play Quiet Tetris

Inspired by classic Tetris progression

Classic Tetris games are satisfying because the challenge grows over time. Quiet Tetris now follows that idea: the game starts at a calmer pace, then increases falling speed as your cleared lines push you into higher levels. The score also rewards line clears more as the level climbs.

What makes it fit Lofi Memory

  • Level, speed, and next-speed progress are visible while you play.
  • The palette stays soft and cozy instead of harsh or overly competitive.
  • You can keep lofi music, notes, journaling, and relaxing games in the same tab.
  • It works as a quick reset when you want a classic arcade puzzle without leaving the calm room.
