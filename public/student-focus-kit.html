Who this is for

They want to begin studying without opening a full productivity system. A calming room, one timer-like tree, and one note area lowers the friction.

2

They already enjoy study-with-me videos, lofi playlists, desk setups, cute timers, and calm browser tools. Lofi Memory gives them a place to use those habits.

3

People who want breaks without scrolling

Short games, a private journal, and the Focus Tree create a cleaner reset than jumping into social media between study blocks.

A simple study loop to promote

Step 1 — Open the room.
Start the lofi music, choose a calm wallpaper, and leave the page visible beside notes or assignments.
Step 2 — Grow the Focus Tree.
Use the Focus room as a visual timer. The longer the student stays with the session, the more the tree grows.
Step 3 — Write one tiny reflection.
After the block, write one sentence in the private journal: what got done, what distracted you, or what should happen next.
Step 4 — Take a clean break.
If the brain feels tired, play one calm game or read one short article, then return to the tree instead of opening a noisy feed.

How to market this to the ICP

TikTok / Reels angle

Show the Focus Tree growing beside study notes. Use hooks like “I built a cozy browser room for people who study better with lofi” or “POV: your study timer grows into a tree.”

Pinterest angle

Create pins around aesthetic study rooms, lofi study tools, cozy digital journaling, and focus timer setups. Link pins to this kit and the study routine article.

Student communities

Share it as a free calm study room in study Discords, Notion/template spaces, productivity forums, and exam-season group chats. Ask for feedback, not just clicks.

Creator collaboration

Offer micro-creators a simple prompt: “Study with this open for 25 minutes and show your tree at the end.” The feature is visual enough for short-form content.

Copy you can use

Short post: I made a cozy lofi study room where your focus tree grows the longer you stay. It has lofi music, notes, private journaling, and tiny no-download games for clean study breaks.

Call to action: Try one 25-minute block: open Lofi Memory, go to Focus, grow the tree, then write one sentence about what you finished.

Why this helps AdSense and SEO too

Instead of adding another thin game page, this creates a stronger product story for the student ICP: a real focus use case, a clear landing page, internal links to the study routine article, and useful copy that explains why the feature exists.
