Lofi Memory
Student study break system

Cozy games, lofi music, and private journaling in one study break tab.

Lofi Memory helps students take softer breaks without falling into noisy feeds: play one relaxing browser game, keep lofi focus music running, and close the reset with a private journal note.

Open Lofi MemoryRead the study break guide

1. Pick a short game

Try Aesthetic Tiles, Snake, Lotus Match, Jigsaw, Sudoku, or a word puzzle when your brain needs a tidy reset.

2. Keep the room calm

Stay inside the same lofi study room instead of opening new tabs, social apps, or high-pressure games.

3. Journal the next step

Write one sentence about what you finished and one sentence about what you will do next.
