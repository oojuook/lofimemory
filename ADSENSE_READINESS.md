# AdSense readiness notes for Lofi Memory

Google states that a site may be considered "not ready to show ads" if ad code is missing or incomplete, the site is unreachable, the site lacks enough unique content or a good user experience, or policy violations are present::cite[453].

Google's ads.txt guidance says ads.txt is optional, but if you use it, the publisher ID must be present and correctly formatted before Google can verify the file::cite[443].

Google Publisher Policies require ad-supported pages to comply with Google's publisher rules and avoid policy-problematic content and behavior::cite[444].

## Project-specific priority actions

1. Reduce reliance on thin or near-duplicate landing pages and concentrate internal linking around stronger, original pages.
2. Keep trust pages easy to find: About, Contact, Privacy, Cookie Policy, Terms, Disclaimer, and Editorial Policy.
3. Keep `public/ads.txt` accurate and live at `/ads.txt`.
4. Leave AdSense script on the main product page, but avoid placing ads on low-value legal or trust pages.
5. Continue growing the article hub with original, useful posts before requesting another review.
