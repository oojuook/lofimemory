# SEO keyword research notes

Search findings show repeated demand patterns around privacy-first journaling, diary locks, mood tracking, journal prompts, and daily diary habits. Competitor snippets commonly use phrases such as "private online diary," "diary with lock," "daily diary," "private journal," "mood tracker," "journal prompts," "photo diary," "gratitude journal," and "online diary app," so these should appear naturally in homepage copy, landing-page cross-links, and metadata::cite[475].

Additional diary-oriented searches show demand for low-friction writing phrases such as "private diary online no signup," "free online diary," "auto-saved in your browser," "daily journal," "mood tracking," and "gratitude prompts." The strongest match for Lofi Memory is to target the low-friction privacy angle without overstating security promises::cite[523].

For games, recurring high-intent phrases include "free browser games," "no download," "no signup," "single player games," "relaxing browser games," "games to play when bored," and exact game modifiers such as "Solitaire online," "Minesweeper online," "Snake game online," "Wordle online," and "Sudoku online." These queries match Lofi Memory's game room and should be represented in the homepage, sitemap, schema, and internal links::cite[493].

Relaxing-game search pages commonly frame the need as soothing puzzles, calming simulations, tranquil play, and no-download installation-free games. Lofi Memory should use "calming games online" and "relaxing games online" in dedicated content while avoiding medical/anxiety claims that sound too strong::cite[363].

For lofi music, search snippets consistently pair lofi with tasks and moods: "lofi beats to study / relax to," "focus music," "chill music," "study music," "reading," "everyday listening," "lofi study music," and "lofi music for studying." Lofi Memory should connect music with journaling, studying, focus, reading, writing, and relaxation rather than only using the generic phrase "lofi music"::cite[497].

Study/focus competitors commonly combine lofi music, Pomodoro or study timers, ambient soundscapes, notes, to-do lists, and time tracking. This supports adding "study timer with music" and "lofi study timer" pages that connect Lofi Memory's music, notes, reminders, and short relaxing game breaks::cite[506].
